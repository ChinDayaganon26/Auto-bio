About Me

I was born on August 26, 2005, at General Hospital in Muñoz, Quezon City. I am a quiet and shy person. I am also an introvert, but sometimes I can be outgoing when I am comfortable with the people around me.

I am currently taking Bachelor of Science in Information Technology. IT was one of my main choices when I entered college. Even though it can be challenging, I am still trying to learn and improve little by little.

I enjoy watching cooking and baking content. I also like learning new things and exploring my interests. I may be shy, but I always try to listen and understand other people.

My Challenges

One of my biggest challenges in school is dealing with activities when I start doing them late. Sometimes I end up cramming because I do not start my tasks early.

Another challenge for me is communicating with people I am not close to. Since I am a shy person, it can be difficult for me to start a conversation or participate confidently, especially during recitations.

These experiences taught me that I still have many things to improve, especially my confidence, communication, and time management.

My Goal

My main goal in college is to finish my degree and graduate. After college, I hope to find a job where I can use what I learned and continue improving my skills.

I am still figuring out what path I want to take in the IT field, but I want to keep learning and find something that I will enjoy doing in the future.

A Memorable Experience

One of my memorable experiences as a student is having to do recitations while feeling very nervous. There are times when I become really anxious when I have to speak in front of the class, especially when I am not prepared or when many people are listening.

Although these moments can be uncomfortable for me, they are also part of my experience as a student. They remind me that I still need to work on my confidence and learn how to express myself better.
